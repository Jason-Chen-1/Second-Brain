Core extracted claims used:

- S22 defines the maximum clique problem as finding a clique with the maximum number of vertices in an undirected graph.
- S22 says the maximum clique problem is NP-hard and the associated decision problem is NP-complete.
- S22 proposes a continuous characterization of the maximum clique problem based on symmetric rank-one nonnegative approximation of a matrix.
- S22 states that local and global minima of the continuous formulation correspond to maximal and maximum cliques.
- S22 reports experiments on 10 randomly generated binary matrices, 36 DIMACS benchmark instances, and 8 CLUTO text-mining data sets.
